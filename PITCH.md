# Angles — pitch

## One line

One stuck thought. Four short angles. Keep it private, or share it.

## The pitch

Angles is an iPhone app for the moment a negative thought gets stuck. You write it down. If it needs a little more context, Angles asks one short follow-up. Then you get the same thought back as four brief takes: Stoic, Hopeful, Witty, and Tough.

Each voice does a different job. Stoic separates what you can still hold from what you cannot. Hopeful finds a real opening already in what you said, without dismissing how hard it is. Witty takes the air out of the moment the way a good friend would, and never jokes at you. Tough names the part you are avoiding and points at the next move. When a voice would land wrong — a joke on grief, toughness when you are already crushed — that angle is left out.

The lines are short, a sentence or two, and written for the thought you actually typed. You can ask for a new take on any one style. The ones you keep live in a private library. Posting one also puts it on Home, where other people can read it, heart the angle that helped, and follow the author. Home opens on For you, with a tab for each style, and you can narrow it by life area and mood.

Sign in with Apple. One free taste, then a subscription.

## What to say out loud

You do not get one generic pep talk. You get four ways of seeing the same moment, short enough to read in a breath, specific to what you wrote. Keep the one that helps. Share it if you want someone else to find it too.
